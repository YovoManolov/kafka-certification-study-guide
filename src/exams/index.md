These are **exam-mode practice interfaces**, designed to feel like a real assessment rather than a Markdown answer sheet.

## How it works

- Start the exam and the timer begins.
- One question is displayed at a time.
- Select one answer before moving on.
- Use **Flag** to mark questions for review.
- The question grid shows answered, unanswered, and flagged questions.
- Answers are hidden until you submit.
- After submission, you get a score, answer-by-answer review, and a list of missed questions.
- Your progress is stored locally in the browser while an exam is in progress.
- No answer data is sent to a server.

> **Practice format:** the timer is a study-mode design, not a claim about the exact delivery format of the official certification exam.

## Recommended workflow

1. Take the exam without notes.
2. Flag uncertain questions instead of stopping to research them.
3. Submit only when you have completed your review.
4. Classify each mistake as **K** (knowledge), **R** (reasoning), **C** (configuration), **T** (trap), **S** (scenario interpretation), or **M** (memory/command).
5. Revisit the corresponding study chapter before retaking the exam.
